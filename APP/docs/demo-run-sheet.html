The Loop, Start to Finish

One establishment, one complaint, one inspection — the full citizen → inspector → admin → public loop, in the order a camera should see it.

~8:00 total run time

Before you hit record

Seed data is fresh Already done this session — npm run seed ran clean: 15 establishments, 45 complaints, 4 users.
Tunnel is live https://absolute-investigated-italia-sailing.trycloudflare.com — load it once on the phone right before recording. It changes if the terminal restarts.
Fresh QR sheet printed or on a second screen Open /admin/qr from the tunnel URL (not localhost) so the codes encode the tunnel address, then screenshot or print the الفرن الذهبي code.
Three browser tabs open on the PC, pre-logged-in Tab 1 — admin (admin@nablus.ps). Tab 2 — inspector (inspector@nablus.ps). Tab 3 — the public establishment page, ready to refresh. Password for all: aman1234.
Phone camera framed on the QR code, ready to scan on cue This is scene 1 — don't scan it before recording starts.

The script

0:00
Scan the QR code Citizen · phone
  • Point the phone camera at the الفرن الذهبي sticker. Tap the notification/link.
  • Phone opens the public page: grade badge, name, address, open-violations count.

This is the only thing on the door — no app to install. Any phone camera reads it.

0:40
Read the public record Citizen · phone
  • Scroll: grade B, last inspection date, score 82/100, the one open violation (cold storage, owner already responded).
  • Scroll to inspection history — grade A→B trend visible.

Everything here traces back to a real inspection. Nothing is typed in by hand.

1:20
Submit a complaint Citizen · phone
  • Tap قدّم شكوى عن هذه المنشأة.
  • Category: حشرات أو قوارض. Description: one real sentence, on camera, live.
  • Attach a photo from the camera roll (EXIF is stripped automatically — worth a line of narration).
  • Submit → reference number appears large on screen. Screenshot it, or read it aloud.

No login, no account. The reference number is the only thing they need to check back later.

2:20
Triage and assign Admin · PC, Tab 1
  • Switch to the admin tab, already on فرز الشكاوى.
  • Refresh — the new complaint is at the top, photo thumbnail visible.
  • Pick سامي عبد الله from the inspector dropdown → إسناد.
  • Green confirmation banner appears.

One click, and it's on a real inspector's queue — not a spreadsheet, not an email.

3:10
Open the inspector's queue Inspector · PC, Tab 2
  • Switch to the inspector tab, جولة اليوم.
  • Point at the summary strip: total assigned, and the مرتفعة / متوسطة / منخفضة counts.
  • الفرن الذهبي is #1 — read one or two of its سبب الترتيب reasons aloud (complaint pressure, prior violation).

The ranking isn't a gut call — every line under it is a real, weighted reason.

3:50
Start the visit — context panel Inspector · PC, Tab 2
  • Tap ابدأ التفتيش.
  • The سياق الزيارة panel opens on top: the open cold-storage violation, and every pending complaint including the one just submitted, photo and all.

Before they've answered a single checklist item, they already know exactly what to check first.

4:40
Walk one section of the checklist Inspector · PC, Tab 2
  • Mark two or three items مطابق, running score updates live in the header.
  • Mark the cold-storage item مخالف — measured value field appears, type 8.
  • Try to advance without a photo — blocked, red notice: a critical failure needs evidence.
  • Attach a photo → unblocked, advance to the next section.

A critical violation without a photo can't leave this screen — that rule is enforced, not just suggested.

5:50
Review and sign Inspector · PC, Tab 2
  • Skip to المراجعة والإرسال (fast-forward the remaining sections beforehand, off-camera).
  • Show the outcome row: previous grade → new grade, score, the one recorded violation with its auto-filled recommendation.
  • Sign in the signature pad.
  • إرسال التفتيش → confirm dialog → confirm.

This is final. Once it's sent, the record is the record — no quiet edits.

6:40
The public page updates itself Citizen · PC Tab 3 or phone
  • Refresh the public establishment page from earlier.
  • Grade badge changed live — B → whatever the new inspection produced. History list grew by one row.

Same public page, no admin step in between. The grade only ever changes because a real inspection happened.

7:15
Close on the dashboard Admin · PC, Tab 1
  • لوحة المعلومات — grade distribution and complaints-over-time both moved.
  • One line to camera: registered establishments, complaints this month, avg. days to close.

One QR code on a door turned into a graded, auditable inspection — start to finish, no paper in between.
